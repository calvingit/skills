# 英文专用规则

编辑英文正文时读取；通用保真、改写与输出要求见 [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md)。

## 禁用词语与搭配

以下表达禁止使用，按完整词语检查，覆盖大小写和常见词形；保护内容及专业术语的例外见入口。

- 装饰性探索与图景：delve、dive deep、embark on a journey、rich tapestry、ever-evolving landscape。
- 宣传与拔高：game-changer、groundbreaking、transformative、revolutionary、unparalleled、world-class、supercharge、unlock the full potential、take it to the next level。
- 模糊褒义与自我评价：seamless、robust、holistic、meticulous、genuinely、quietly powerful、in-depth analysis、comprehensive guide、everything you need to know。
- 万能意义与成功宣言：a testament to、pivotal role、indelible mark、stay ahead of the curve、turn challenges into opportunities、drive meaningful impact、the future is full of possibilities。
- 空泛提醒与助手套话：it is worth noting、it is important to note、needless to say、I hope this helps、happy to help、feel free to ask、without further ado。

robust regression 是专业术语，不因 robust 命中禁词而改动。

## 禁用句式

以下句式及同构变体禁止使用。

- 对照反转：It's not X, it's Y；This isn't about X, it's about Y；Not X, but Y；Not X, just Y；No X, just Y。
- 递进拔高：Not only X, but also Y；It's more than just X；It doesn't just X, it Y；This is not the end, but the beginning。
- 设问自答：Why? Because…；The answer? X；Is it perfect? No；Does it matter? Absolutely。
- 宏大开场：In today's fast-paced world…；In an ever-evolving landscape…；In an era defined by…。
- 内容预告与自我标榜：Let's dive in；Let's unpack this；In this comprehensive guide, we will…；Buckle up；Here's everything you need to know。
- 自动升华：This serves as a powerful reminder that…；This marks a new chapter in…；The possibilities are endless；Only time will tell。
- 空泛夸奖与预设需求：Great question；You're absolutely right；What you really need is…；The fact that you're asking this means…。

改写保留排除关系：“The failure was not caused by a timeout, but by denied access”可改为“Denied access caused the failure; a timeout did not cause it”，不能只留下 denied access。

## 结构与句法

- 删除空转的 In conclusion、In summary、Overall，保留独立结论；不用 On the one hand…on the other hand…强行配两面或凑三项排比。
- 清理 in order to、due to the fact that、in terms of、when it comes to 等冗长句法，保留目的、因果和限定关系。
- 检查句尾 highlighting、underscoring、showcasing 等分词从句是否只有泛泛意义；保留独立事实、推断及 resulting in 等真实结果。
- is、are、has 已准确表达时，不刻意换成 serves as、stands as、boasts；后者确有独立含义时保留。
- 按文体处理主动／被动语态，不统一改成主动句，不补造执行者。沿用英式／美式拼写和正式程度，不强制缩写。
- may slightly reduce 中 may 限定可能性，slightly 限定幅度，不能为只留一个限定词而删除其中任何一个。
- 不机械统一句首、句长、段形，不重复加粗标签；保留必要的列表、强调、括号和破折号，不按数量删标点。
- experts say、studies show 缺少来源时按入口处理，不作为权威铺垫。
